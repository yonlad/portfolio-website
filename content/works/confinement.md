A welded steel frame that gestures at once toward a baby's cradle and a military field stretcher. Its bars are partly wrapped in blank Israeli shiva mourning notices — the printed death announcements that go up in the street after someone dies — with the name-field left empty on every sheet. The piece sits on the floor.
