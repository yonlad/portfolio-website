Yonatan Eshban-Laderman lives and works in Brooklyn, NY. He holds a BS and an MA from Stanford University. His practice moves across sculpture, installation, new media, and performance, built from research into militarization, surveillance technology, and the material infrastructures that connect civilian and military life. His work has been shown at CCRMA and Stanford's Elam Theater, SOMArts Cultural Center (San Francisco), NYCResistor (Brooklyn), and Pop Gun Art Gallery (New York), where he wrote and directed a commissioned performance. His critical writing has appeared in the Brooklyn Rail, IMPULSE Magazine, Ha'aretz, Local Call, and Artefuse.
